## MODULE BOARD
同じ材料: `You move two pieces forward.`

- 肯定: `You move two pieces forward.`
- できる: `You can move two pieces forward.`
- できますか: `Can you move two pieces forward?`
- 普通の疑問: `Do you move two pieces forward?`
- 否定: `You do not move two pieces forward.`
- 何を: `What do you move forward?`
- いくつ: `How many pieces do you move forward?`
