## MODULE LADDER
同じ素材を固定して、操作だけ変える。

`You order two tuna sandwiches.`

1. 肯定 → `You order two tuna sandwiches.`
2. できる → `You can order two tuna sandwiches.`
3. できますか → `Can you order two tuna sandwiches?`
4. 普通の疑問 → `Do you order two tuna sandwiches?`
5. 否定 → `You do not order two tuna sandwiches.`
6. What → `What do you order?`
7. How many → `How many tuna sandwiches do you order?`
