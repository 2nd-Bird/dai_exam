# 英語 DAY2 — Are you...? / Do you...?

INDEX: E02

## 型
- `I am ...` → `Are you ...?`
- `I like/play/practice ...` → `Do you ...?`
- Are → `Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.`
- Do → `Yes, I do. / No, I don't.`

## A
1. I am a basketball fan. → ______ you a basketball fan?
2. I like music. → ______ you like music?
3. I play soccer. → ______ you play soccer?
4. I practice soccer every day. → ______ you practice soccer every day?
5. I am a hip-hop dancer. → 質問文へ。
6. I like fish. → 質問文へ。
7. I play games. → 質問文へ。
8. Do you like fish? → 自分で短答。
9. Do you play games? → 自分で短答。
10. Are you a basketball fan? → 自分で短答。

## B

12. Do you like hip-hop? - Yes, I ______.
13. Are you a basketball fan? - No, I'm ______.
14. Do you practice soccer every day? - No, I ______.
